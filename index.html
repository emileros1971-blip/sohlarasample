Yoga, breathwork, and meditation every morning

A five-day reset that teaches you to work with your nervous system

At Sohlara, we use guided breathwork, gentle movement, and time to rest to help you tune in to the signals your body sends. Over five days in Tenerife, you’ll practise noticing changes in your breathing, where you hold tension, and what it feels like when your body begins to settle.

Your nervous system connects your brain, your body, and the world around you. It shapes how you respond to stress, experience emotions, and connect with others. During the retreat, we’ll help you use what you notice in each session to find practices you can return to when you need a moment to reset at home.

01

Recognise how stress shows up in your body

A tight jaw or shallow breathing can tell you a lot about how you’re feeling. In guided breathwork, practise noticing those changes and find a breathing exercise you can use when pressure builds.

02

Find a moment before you react

When an email or a difficult conversation puts you on edge, it can be hard to pause. At Sohlara, practise using your breath and movement to give yourself a moment to choose how you respond.

03

Give yourself time to wind down

Spend five days in Tenerife with gentle movement, guided breathwork, and quiet time between sessions. Explore what helps you shift out of work mode and settle into rest.

04

Build a practice you can keep

Try simple breathing and movement exercises with our team. Pick one that feels right for you and practise it during the retreat so you can use it again when you get home.

05

Catch the early signs of tension

Your shoulders may tighten before you realise how much you’re carrying. In guided movement sessions, learn to spot those signals and explore what helps you ease the tension.

06

Make room to feel more at ease

If the workday follows you home in the form of stiff shoulders or a racing mind, give yourself five days with gentle yoga, outdoor time, and room to rest. See what helps you feel more at home in your body.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you offer stays longer than 5 days? +

Absolutely! Sohlara operates year-round, 7 days a week, with an open-retreat format that allows you to arrive on any day and stay for as long as you wish. For longer stays or tailored arrangements, email us at contact@sohlararetreats.com or call us on +34 690 227 210.

Can I come alone? +

Of course! Many of our guests travel solo. Our retreats are designed to be social, welcoming and inclusive, offering plenty of opportunities to connect with like-minded people while leaving you the freedom to enjoy time to yourself.

Which airport should I fly into? +

The most convenient airport is Tenerife South Airport (TFS), which is a 20 minute drive from our retreat location.

Do you cater to different dietary requirements? +

All our meals are vegetarian or vegan. If you have any allergies or specific dietary requirements, please let us know in your booking form so we can discuss the necessary adjustments with you in advance.
