Casa Back to Origins · Between North & South

Sohlara Stone House

Less noise. More nature. True serenity. A charming eco-home where simplicity becomes a luxury of its own, nestled between the lush north and the sunlit south of Tenerife.

Book your stay View gallery

The house

Escape the Noise, Embrace the Moment

Tenerife is more than luxury resorts and ancient caves — it is also about finding harmony between the past and present, between comfort and authenticity. Nestled between the lush north and the rugged south, Casa Back to Origins is a place where simplicity becomes a luxury of its own.

This cozy 3-bedroom home is designed with an eco-conscious spirit, blending natural materials, soft earth tones, and an effortless, inviting charm. It is not extravagant, nor primitive — it is exactly what you need to disconnect from the modern world and reconnect with yourself.

  • Minimalist yet warm, with handcrafted wooden details
  • Built with sustainability in mind, in harmony with the surroundings
  • A cozy retreat, perfect for unwinding after a day of exploration
  • Outdoor spaces designed for relaxation, with peaceful views of the landscape

Your stay

A Home That Feels Like Freedom

Here, there are no distractions — just open skies, fresh air, and the rhythm of nature. Wake up to the sound of birds, spend the afternoon reading in a hammock, and let the sunset be your evening entertainment.

A perfect balance

If Sohlara Villa in the north is all about refined tradition and Sohlara Caves in the south takes you back to ancient times, Casa Back to Origins is the bridge between the two. It offers a connection to nature without sacrificing comfort, making it the perfect place for those who seek simplicity, authenticity, and tranquility.

Location

Between the vibrant greenery of the north and the sun-drenched landscapes of the south, Casa Back to Origins is the perfect middle ground — secluded enough for peace, yet close enough to explore the best of both worlds.
