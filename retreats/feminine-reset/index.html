A Retreat for the Modern Woman

FEMININE RESET

Recharge Your Body. Calm Your Mind. Reclaim Your Energy.

26 February – 3 March 2027 • Tenerife

Six days to slow down enough to hear yourself again.

Book your place now

Make space for yourself

Your career is moving forward. The people you love know they can count on you. And even on a rare morning with nothing in the diary, part of you is already thinking about what comes next. It can be hard to admit how tired you are when so much of your life is going well.

Feminine Reset gives you six days in Tenerife to step out of that rhythm. Guided breathwork, gentle movement and mindfulness help you notice what your body has been asking for. Sessions on women’s health, lifestyle medicine and nervous system regulation offer practical ways to carry what you discover into everyday life.

Reserve your place

What to expect at the retreat

Feel more at ease in your body

Guided breathwork, gentle movement and body-awareness exercises give you time to notice where you hold tension and what helps you ease it.

You’ll practise simple ways to respond to those signals and find a pace that feels good to you.

Give yourself time to rest

It can take time for your mind to quieten, even when you finally have a day to yourself. Floating therapy, sauna time, guided meditation and quiet moments outdoors give you several ways to slow down.

Notice what helps you unwind so you can make more room for it at home.

Feel more like yourself in your relationships

When you’re stretched thin, it’s easy to say yes before you’ve had time to consider what you need.

We’ll explore how stress affects the way you communicate and practise expressing your needs and setting boundaries with warmth and clarity.

The location

A private villa in northern Tenerife

Stay in La Orotava, where green hills look out over the Atlantic and Mount Teide rises inland.

Between sessions, take time for yourself in the villa’s jacuzzi, sauna or flotation tank.

Optional outings take you to Mount Teide, Puerto de la Cruz and the natural volcanic pools of Tenerife’s north coast.
