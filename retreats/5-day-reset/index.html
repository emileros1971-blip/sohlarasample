Want to Explore More of Tenerife?

We offer a range of optional excursions at an additional cost, giving you the freedom to choose the experiences that speak to you.

  • Masca Valley — Discover one of Tenerife's most spectacular mountain villages and landscapes.
  • Coastal Hike & Beach Day — Walk from La Caleta to Diego Hernández Naturist Beach, also known as Hippie Beach.
  • Barranco del Infierno — Hike Tenerife's famous Hell's Ravine.
  • Puerto de la Cruz & Natural Pools — Explore the north of Tenerife and visit Charco de La Laja and Charco del Viento.
  • Los Gigantes & Whale Watching — Admire the dramatic cliffs and discover Tenerife's marine life.
  • Loro Parque — Visit Tenerife's renowned animal and nature park.
  • Abama Beach Day — Enjoy a relaxing day by the ocean.

Discover the Healing Experiences Available at Sohlara:

1. Body & Nervous System

Therapeutic Massage
A personalised massage session designed to relieve muscle tension, ease physical discomfort and promote deep relaxation. Stressful and traumatic experiences can influence how we hold tension in our bodies. Through mindful, therapeutic touch, this session offers space to soften physical tension, reconnect with your body and support a deeper sense of safety, relaxation and wellbeing.

Somatic Work + Cold Plunge + Sauna
A guided experience combining somatic movement, cold-water immersion and sauna. Explore body awareness, your responses to physical stress and the contrast between heat and cold, while practising mindful presence and learning to approach challenging sensations with greater awareness.

1:1 Personalised Nervous System Regulation Plan
A private one-to-one session to explore your stress patterns, understand your nervous system and identify your individual needs. Includes practical exercises such as breathwork, grounding and somatic techniques, plus a personalised daily practice plan to help you manage stress and cultivate greater calm and emotional balance.

2. Personalised Wellbeing

Personalised 30-Day Nutrition & Diet Plan
Our on-site doctor, Amandah, will conduct a one-to-one consultation to understand your health history, lifestyle, nutritional needs and personal goals. Based on your individual needs, she will create a personalised 30-day nutrition plan designed to support your overall wellbeing, help you build sustainable eating habits and give you practical guidance you can incorporate into your everyday life.

30-Day Personalised Herbal Detox & Parasite Cleanse Programme
A personalised programme incorporating herbal products and guidance to support your digestive wellbeing and overall health. Includes the materials needed for the first week, with recommendations tailored to your individual needs and guidance on how to follow the programme over 30 days.

Bioresonance Assessment
A holistic assessment exploring your overall wellbeing, energetic balance and chakras using bioresonance techniques. The session aims to identify areas that may benefit from further attention and support your personalised wellness journey.

Bioresonance Therapy
Following the bioresonance assessment, we will conduct a personalised therapy session using bioresonance techniques, designed to restore energetic balance and overall wellbeing.

3. Emotional Exploration & Ritual

Sacred Fire & Rapé Ceremony
A ceremonial experience combining the sacred fire with the traditional use of Rapé, a plant-based snuff originating in Indigenous Amazonian traditions. This guided ritual offers space for intention-setting, reflection and connection with the present moment, inviting you to slow down and approach your inner experience with awareness and respect.

1:1 Recall Healing & New Germanic Medicine Session
A personalised one-to-one session exploring the connections between emotional experiences, personal history and your wellbeing. Through guided conversation and reflection, you'll explore recurring emotional patterns and gain insights to support greater self-awareness and personal growth.
