The invitation

More of less.

We spend much of our lives responding to what is happening around us: messages, expectations, responsibilities, other people’s opinions, the pressure to achieve, buy, improve and keep moving.

For six days, we are stepping away from some of that.

Back to Stone is a retreat built around observation, honest conversations, meditation, body awareness and time in nature. It is an opportunity to look more closely at how we live, how we relate to others, what drives our decisions and where we may have stopped listening to ourselves.

Sohlara · South Tenerife

The caves and the land

Our retreat takes place in a stone house and natural caves in the south of Tenerife, surrounded by rugged volcanic land and open skies.

Here, stepping away from constant stimulation has a physical setting. There’s open space to walk, stone walls around us when we gather, and somewhere quiet to sit alone.

The property has indoor and outdoor practice spaces, a sauna, a jacuzzi and cold-water facilities.

What we’ll explore

The things we rarely stop to look at.

Truth and the games we play

When do I say what someone wants to hear?

We’ll look at the little bargains we make for approval, and the stories we tell ourselves to keep the peace.

Observing before reacting

A thought appears. A feeling follows. The body responds.

In meditation we’ll practise noticing that sequence, especially when irritation or fear makes us want to act straight away.

Boundaries and intuition

What does a genuine yes feel like?

We’ll pay attention to the moments when our words say one thing and our bodies tell us something else.

Habits and enough

What am I reaching for, and what do I actually need?

Food, a purchase, another hour on a screen. We’ll notice what draws us to familiar comforts and what it feels like to pause.

You can explore any of these questions quietly. Sharing with the group is always optional.

Our days together

Space for what emerges.

Some sessions will take place together. Others will give you space to work at your own pace or individually with Monika. There will be time to sit with yourself and see everyday life from a little distance.

Depending on the group, the setting and the weather, our programme may include the following.

  • Guided meditation and periods of silence
  • Workshops and reflective exercises
  • Conversations around relationships, boundaries and personal patterns
  • Body-awareness practices and gentle movement
  • Breathwork and sound journeys
  • Time for rest, solitude and individual reflection

31 December

New Year’s Eve

By New Year’s Eve, we’ll have spent a few days getting to know the place and each other. We’ll have the opportunity to watch the celebrations and fireworks from a distance.

Fire, desert, stones.

And stillness in the middle of an overstimulating world.

We leave on 2 January, with the possibility of extending your stay if accommodation is available.

Your facilitator

Monika

“I am interested in the things that often go unnoticed, or noticed and then ignored.”

I’m a coach, alternative therapist and trainer in authentic communication. I’m also an astrologer and a mother.

For over ten years I’ve explored psychosomatics and psychobiology. I’ve learned the most from living, observing relationships and asking questions. Here, I’ll guide you through that kind of observation, together and individually.

During your stay

Food and everyday habits

Food options

Food will follow the same principle of simplicity as the rest of the retreat. We’ll use fresh, minimally processed ingredients and serve them simply.

Before arrival, we’ll ask about your preferred food option. The choices below depend on individual needs and final arrangements.

  • Cooked vegan meals, simply prepared with minimally processed ingredients
  • Raw food
  • Fruit-based meals
  • Optional fasting, subject to individual suitability and separate safety guidelines

A shared approach to consumption

Our exploration of consumption goes beyond food. It also includes the everyday stimulants, habits and distractions that shape our routines.

During the retreat, we will have shared agreements around caffeine, alcohol, smoking and other intoxicating substances.

Time away from screens

We encourage you to put your phone aside as much as possible. If you need to work or stay in touch with family, let us know before arrival so we can agree on times that work for you.
